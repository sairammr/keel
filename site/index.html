A hidden threshold
isn’t hidden.

Every act-or-hold decision leaks one bit. Three actions locate a fixed trigger to ±0.005 HF — and the public reserve turns it into a stop-hunt target.

  • Acted at 1.09 → trigger below. Held at 1.06 → above. A binary search someone else runs against you.
  • The whole game is a $205 window. HF 1.114 at $2,000; liquidation at $1,795.
  • balanceOf was public all along. Located trigger + known reserve = drained defence.

So the trigger refuses
to be a number.

trigger = clamp(base + k·σ + jitter(HMAC(salt, round)))

Fresh draw, every round

Keyed by a sealed salt and the round index. No draw repeats; none is predictable.

Upward only

Jitter can fire the defence earlier, never later. Cleverness never costs survival.

Hard floor beneath it all

A fixed floor and an emergency override at HF 1.03. Survival by construction.
